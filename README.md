# Budget Tracker

A simple and intuitive budget tracking application to help you manage your finances effectively. Track your income and expenses, visualize your spending habits, and stay on top of your financial goals.



## Features

- **Income and Expense Tracking**: Easily add and categorize your income and expenses.
- **Budget Management**: Set monthly budgets and track your progress.
- **Responsive Design**: Access your budget tracker on any device, desktop or mobile.
- **Data Persistence**: Your data is saved locally, so you can pick up where you left off.
